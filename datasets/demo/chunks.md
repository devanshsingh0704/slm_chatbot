# Demo dataset: Example Metalworks Ltd. (a fictional company)
Everything below is invented for testing the pipeline. Emails use the reserved example.com domain and phone
numbers the 555-01xx range reserved for fiction.

## D01 | Company overview
sources: demo company profile
Example Metalworks Ltd. makes cold-roll-formed steel profiles, welded steel tubes, and ready-to-fit brackets for machine builders, furniture makers, and solar-mounting companies. It works from customer drawings to finished, coated parts.

## D02 | Company facts: legal name, founding year, owner, number of employees, head office
sources: demo company profile
Legal name: Example Metalworks Ltd. Founded in 1987. Owner and Managing Director: Maria Lindqvist. Number of employees: about 120. Head office: the Riverside plant (see locations).

## D06 | Products, materials, and capacity
sources: demo product sheet
Products: open roll-formed profiles, round and square welded tubes, brackets, and solar-mounting rails. Materials: cold-rolled and galvanised steel from 0.8 mm to 4 mm thick. The plant runs 6 roll-forming lines and 2 tube mills. Parts can be powder coated in 12 standard colours.

## D07 | Quality checks and certifications
sources: demo quality manual
Every order gets a first-piece check, in-process dimension checks every 30 minutes, and a final visual and dimensional inspection before dispatch. A coordinate measuring machine (CMM) is used for complex brackets. One source claims ISO 9001 certification, but no certificate is published, so it is unverified.

## D08 | Delivery and order rules
sources: demo sales terms
Minimum order: 500 metres of profile or 200 parts. Standard lead time is 3 to 4 weeks after drawing approval. Prices are quoted per order only and are not published.
